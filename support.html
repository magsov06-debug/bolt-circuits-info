Bolt Circuits Support

Need help or found a bug? Email boltcircuits@outlook.com and include your device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. If you delete the app or reset your device, the progress is lost.

The app is stuck or a level does not work.
Close the app completely and open it again. If the problem remains, email us with the level name.

Does the app need internet?
No. The game runs offline. The font may fall back to a system font without internet.

Does the app collect my data?
No. Read the Privacy Policy.
