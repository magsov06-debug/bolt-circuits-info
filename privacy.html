Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy applies to the app Bolt Circuits (bundle ID com.magnussovik.bolt), developed by Magnus Søvik, Norway, who is responsible for the processing described here.

Summary

Bolt Circuits is free and shows ads. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. We do not run our own servers, and we have no accounts and no analytics. The one in-app purchase, Remove Ads, is handled entirely by Apple. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ad service needs to show and measure ads, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows full-screen ads between levels, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to unlock a hint. These ads come from Google AdMob, run by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, plus other device identifiers
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • your IP address, and from it an approximate location (country or city level, not GPS)
  • interactions with ads, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information from the ad software

Google uses this data according to its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google may share it with its advertising partners.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you open the app, you are asked whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. You can say yes or no. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • United States: depending on your state, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for targeted ads in the message shown in the app.
  • Tracking (iOS): the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • Change your mind: where the law requires it, Settings in the app has a Privacy choices button that opens the consent form again. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. It turns off the full-screen ads and opens every level's solution without a video. The purchase is processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; we receive no payment details and no personal information. Whether you own it is read from your Apple ID through the App Store and remembered on your device, so it follows you to new devices and can be restored with the Restore Purchases button in Settings.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as completed levels, unlocked hints, sound on/off, scraps, upgrades and chosen looks) are saved locally on your device. We never receive this data, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Other third-party services

The app loads the font Chakra Petch from Google Fonts. When your device fetches the font, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address.

Children

Bolt Circuits is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We ourselves collect no personal information from anyone; ad-related data is handled by Google as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold any of your data ourselves, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; you are also welcome to contact us. In Norway you can complain to Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
