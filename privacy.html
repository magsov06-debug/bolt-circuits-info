Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy applies to the app Bolt Circuits (bundle ID com.magnussovik.bolt), developed by Magnus Søvik.

Data we collect

None. Bolt Circuits does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as completed levels, sound on/off and worlds you build in the Playground) are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Third-party services

The app loads the font Chakra Petch from Google Fonts. When your device fetches the font, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. We do not send any other data to Google or anyone else.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
